## What the user did (seen) and why (guess)

1. **Order is the story's order, not the download order.** The files are numbered 1–4; the cut
   plays 4 → 3 → 2 → 1, so part 1 comes before part 2. (guess: the numbers are newest-first.)
2. **The hook comes from the first story and then the show sting**, the same opening shape as the
   daily long form (hook → `SFX_โหนกระแส` + BGM `Where Everything Began` → story).
3. **Each video keeps 15–20 %** (about 3.5 min of 20). Pieces are long (median ~8 s, many 15–43 s):
   a compilation keeps whole statements, not micro-cuts.
4. **Each story ends on its source video's own ending** (A2 pieces at src 1154 / 1193 / 1166, near
   each video's end), and a cloth-whoosh SFX (`Apparel Flapping…`) marks every change of story
   (19.5, 22.5, 258.9, 446.4, 656.2). (guess: the viewer needs a sound cue that a new case starts,
   because the studio looks the same in all four.)
5. Title theme: the four cases share one idea (old age / care homes, "บั้นปลายชีวิต"), so the
   compilation is themed, not just "best of".
