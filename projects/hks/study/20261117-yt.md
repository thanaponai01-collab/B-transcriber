## Principles behind it (the why)

1. **The hook is the case file, and the body pays it back in order** (seen). Five bites in story
   order, every one replayed later, and the last one is also the ending.
2. **Open on a question the viewer already has** (seen 24.4). The host's "did you know her?" sets
   up the body instead of a guest sentence or the host summary. This is ask, then tell, used as an
   opening.
3. **Keep the investigators' reasoning when there is no eyewitness** (seen, the why is a guess). The
   host's timeline maths ("40 นาที", 245–252; "ห่างกันแค่ 7 นาที", 286.9) and the lawyer's
   deductions stay, although rule 3 cuts host opinions. Nobody saw the fall (her own clip says she
   found him gone), so the reasoning *is* the story.
4. **Keep many "we are not accusing anyone" lines** (seen 424.1, 497.0, 536.3, 587.0, 865.4,
   1127.4; one at src ≈3726 is dropped). **guess:** this is legal cover where the cut points at a living woman and her ex.
5. **Keep a retelling when the differences between tellings are the evidence; drop a second-hand
   one** (guess). Her account is told twice, first as the police statement (386.9) and then in her
   own voice (605–766). The second clip (780–855) then questions it with new facts: why she opened
   the door, the ex's temper, Boom hiding his things at 1:45. The exact times are what the host
   doubts (855.6). The friend's retelling (T22) and the host's summaries (T0, T6, T23) go.
6. **Keep the conclusion, drop the demonstration** (seen). The 2-min live phone test (T12) goes, but
   its finding (busy = collision or a call in progress, 451.8–467.0) stays.
7. **Words keep broadcast order when the broadcast already runs in clock order; pictures move to
   the words** (seen). Compare 20261123, where the words moved.
8. **Dense where a still or insert hides the cuts, whole where people reason on camera** (seen).
   The last 4.5 min are long pieces: the police, the lawyer, the father.
9. **Hold the outgoing shot across a sound jump** (seen 86.5, 146.6, 1032.1, 1151.3). This is a short
   picture hold, not B-roll.
10. **The ending is the hook's last promise** (seen here; guess as a rule).
