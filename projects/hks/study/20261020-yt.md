## Principles (the why)

1. **The hook sells the spectacle and the debunk, not the plot** (seen: no money in the hook
   voice). Each hook line is a promise the body keeps by replaying it (seen, all 11).
2. **Threads, not broadcast order.** One reorder only, and it moves the conflict to open the money
   thread (seen 398.5). *(guess on the why)*
3. **Each point once, in its best version** (seen): the doctor's T9 vessel-line point over T4's;
   the host's one-line chain of four masters (195.4) over the guest's slow list; T12's clean "several
   buyers make 3 million" over T11's nine muddled minutes. Rule 1's "host summary if the guest is slow"
   applied mid-story.
4. **Cut hard only where the picture hides it** (seen): dense under item close-ups and under a
   still screenshot in the source; long and whole where there is nothing to show (57.9 s, 42.6 s).
5. **Ask, then tell** (seen): host questions open beats: 57.3 "เรื่องราวมันเกิดอะไรขึ้น", 110.2
   "เอามาจากไหน", 236.0 "ทำไมคุณถึงไปซื้อ", 417.3 "เรียกเงินคุณเท่าไร", 992.2 "กลัวไหม".
6. **Let the accused answer, and keep their repeated excuse** (seen: 106 s and 119 s of calls, both
   ending on "ศิลปะ", 757.3 and 869.4 with the host's "งานศิลปะอีกแล้วเหรอ"). Why (guess): fairness,
   and the echo is the punchline: every master says "art". Phrom's "500 บาท" vs "หลักหมื่น" (828.2)
   is the debunk in one number.
7. **A joke stays only if it lands on the story's verdict** (seen): ดาวจ๊อก, กราม, เงาะ, abalone are
   gone; "เสกหนังควายเข้าท้องพี่หมี" (hook 19.5, body 355.5) mocks the masters and stays. *(guess)*
8. **Law short and late, then the guest's resolve** (seen 896.9–1003.6; law 95 s of 984). Same as
   20261123.
9. **Feelings that don't move events go** (seen: "มันเจ็บไหม — มากค่ะ", src 2914–2950, cut).
