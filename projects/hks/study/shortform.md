# FB 3-min and 1-min cuts across 12 episodes (overnight study)

How the user's `2 FACEBOOK` (1080², just under 3:00) and `3 SQUARE SELECT` (just under 1:00, then
nested into `4 REEL SIZE` with the reel frame) are cut, for every daily episode. Read offline with
`output/hks_study/study_short.py`: each kept piece mapped back to where it sits in that day's YT cut
(`<day>.short.txt`; summary from `short_summary.py`). The 20261120 workflow
([../workflow-log.md](../workflow-log.md) Steps 6–8) already records the user's own rules: same hook
as YT, main story only, 2:59:24, no end logo, built by copying the YT cut. This file is what holds
across episodes. Tags: **seen** / **guess**; nothing confirmed.

## What holds in every episode (seen, 12/12)

1. **Both short cuts are made from the finished YT cut, not from the footage.** Only 20261001 has
   any piece the YT cut lacks (3.8 s). The YT long form is the master; the shorts are selections.
2. **Same hook as YT, to the frame** (hook end equal in YT, FB and 1-min every day).
3. **The FB body is a digest of the whole long form, in YT order.** 150–158 s of body taken as 6–14
   runs spread from the YT's first minutes to its last. The only backward jumps are to hook lines
   (a hook line replayed in its story place, e.g. 20261123's stabbing at FB 146.9).
4. **The 1-min body is one beat: 33–44 s, 1–4 runs.** It is not a mini-digest. It is the climax, a
   confrontation, or one shocking fact.
5. **The reels are wrappers, never re-cut** (seen 12/12): `4 REEL SIZE` = `3 SQUARE SELECT` nested
   under `PSD-REEL/1.png`; `5 REEL 1-3 SIZE` = `2 FACEBOOK D1 Copy 01` (the FB without its square
   logo) nested under the same frame. 20261109 adds a second overlay `2.png` (not opened).
6. **Lengths:** FB 168–180 s (four days at exactly 179.96), 1-min 57–63 s (the "just under" rule
   with a few misses: 20261003 at 63.04, 20261002 at 60.00).

## The why (guess)

7. **The FB ending is "strong and aggressive"** (the user's own words for 20261120, seen across the
   days): a defiant line, an accusation, a threat, or the lawyer's hard verdict. When the YT ending
   is already like that, the FB reuses it (1001, 1002, 1020, 1112). When the YT ends calmly
   (1123: reassurance), the FB stops earlier, on the last hard line.
8. **The 1-min is the FB's punch, with nothing before it but the hook** (in 6 of 12 it ends on the
   same line as the FB). Otherwise it is the single most shareable fact (1001 "4 hours", 1002
   "40,000 households", 1110 "200 baht, 86 years"). Why: in 33 s after the hook there is room for
   one turn, and a Reel viewer needs a reaction, not a story. The reel headline is not that line: on six days
   (1001, 1012, 1110, 1117, 1118, 1123, `PSD-REEL/1.png`) it states the **premise** in three lines
   (a shout in red, the case, the twist: "แม่ลูกขายดอกไม้ / โดนแก๊งผู้กำกับละคร อ้างเป็น ตร. /
   อุ้มรีดเงินกว่า 5 แสน!"). So the reel frame gives the premise and the 1-min gives the punch; 20261120's
   "เช็กเกลียว" headline matching its last line is that day's premise being the provocation itself.
9. **Issue days end the 1-min on a number.** The FB ends on advice or a call to act, the 1-min on
   the number that shocks (1001, 1002, 1110). That fits HKS-11 (the day's type sets the shape).

## Could be better (guess)

- **The 1-min reuses the hook's lines in the body** (1117, 1123): in 60 s the viewer hears the same
  line twice. A 1-min body could take its beat from the YT's middle instead.
- **Two 1-min cuts are over 59.96 s** (1002 60.00, 1003 63.04): against the "just under" rule, if
  that rule also holds for the 1-min (ask).
