## The why (guess)

1. **A theme, not just four cases.** The title card promises one feeling (ช้ำ) and every case is one
   version of it. EP.167 is a saga (two parts of one care-home story plus two related). The viewer is
   sold a mood, so case order is free; EP.162 plays its files 1→4, EP.167 plays 4→1 (part 1 before
   part 2). The numbering is not the rule; the story is.
2. **Equal shares** (204–228 s, about 20 % of each source): no case dominates; the compilation is a
   menu.
3. **The hook is one case's peak**, the strongest confrontation, and its lines come back in that case
   (106–128 s and 211–225 s), as in the dailies (HKS-2).
4. **Each case runs: premise in the first line → money / evidence → the scene of discovery → the other
   side's short answer → outcome.** No host intro, no studio greetings (rule 1).
5. **Each case closes on a button and the source runs on under the card**: case 1 keeps 21 s of the
   guest's own last message on A2 before the card, case 4 the broadcast's goodbye. Words checked
   inside each card window: only silence or "สวัสดีครับ". The A2 tail runs on 0.5–3.5 s past the card,
   silent, under the next case's start. EP.167 does the same with quiet tails.
