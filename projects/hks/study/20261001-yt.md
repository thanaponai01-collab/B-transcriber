## Principles behind it (the why)

1. **The viewer's question is "does it reach me, and how deep?"** (seen in what's kept, guess as the why). Every
   province beat keeps the place + yes/no + centimetres line (Nakhon Sawan "ไม่หนัก", Lopburi "ไม่", Bangkok 40–50 cm
   under the wall, Sena +20–30 cm, Chachoengsao town 50 cm) and drops the m³/s arithmetic.
2. **Each point once, and the clearest telling wins, wherever it sits** (seen: T7 dropped for the 1757 restatement;
   Top's first telling at 3873 dropped for the cleaner one at 3900).
3. **A host explanation stays only when it answers the question just raised, with something to look at** (seen: the
   host's hand-on-map dam explanation 361–402, with the director confirming, is kept, and so is the host's plain
   "อย่าตกหน้าเขื่อน" restatement in the hook; the host's general lectures at 857–990 and 1982–2196 are dropped; guess on the rule).
4. **Cut hard under the map, keep people whole** (seen). It's the 20261123 lesson again, now with a pattern: the authority's map talk
   gets tightened, the witnesses' own stories don't.
5. **Threat first, then hope** (seen in the order of the hook and the arc; guess as intent). The hook (dam risk → kid's app) is the
   whole cut in miniature: 0–453 risk, 594–920 the citizen answer, 1046 the unity line.
6. **A guest's real-world stake is kept even inside a data segment** (seen: "จุดศูนย์รวมน้ำเนี่ยมันคือบ้านผมเอง", 318.3).
   It's the one personal line in the first 450 s.
7. **No reorder without a waiting viewer** (guess, from the absence of reorders).
