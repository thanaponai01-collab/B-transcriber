## Principles behind it (the why)

1. **Backstory is kept only as motive, at triple speed** (seen 18.6–151): factory, shares, rent.
   Everything the abduction needs, nothing else. *(guess: rule 2's "drop backstory" means
   "compress backstory to the motive".)*
2. **The hook is the ordeal in her own voice; the cards carry the facts** (seen 0–18.6).
3. **Evidence before denial, denial before verdict** (seen, reorders 2–3). For a he-said-she-said
   segment the order is built like a trial, not like the broadcast.
4. **A doubt is closed where it is raised** (seen, reorder 1).
5. **Ask, then tell** (seen): host questions open beats ("เกิดอะไรขึ้นครับ" 155.9, "แต่ละวันเขาให้เราทำ
   อะไรครับ" 352.4, "สภาพร่างกายเป็นอย่างไรบ้างครับ" 513.7, "มูลเหตุจูงใจ…เพราะอะไร" 667.1).
6. **A host opinion stays when it is the episode's thesis** (seen 981.3, 31.9 s "ช่องว่างทางกฎหมาย …
   ขังตายไปเลย 2 ปี 3 ปี"; 1147.8 "delete ชีวิตคน"). Opinions that only react or excuse ("one side
   only", T20) go. This departs from rule 3. *(guess for the line between them)*
7. **Masks and silhouettes are covers** (seen): her own shot a few seconds away, or a phone
   silhouette, carries tight cuts.
8. **Picture proves the words when a picture exists, otherwise let the speaker hold** (seen).
9. **The show's own process is not the story** (seen in the drops: CCTV request, inspectors, news
   team, licence talk, "tomorrow").
10. **Law short and late, from an authority** (seen 1059.8, 26.7 s); 20261123 again.
