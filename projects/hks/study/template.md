## What the daily cuts use (seen, 12 YT cuts 1001–1123)

- **Every day (12/12):** the hook stack (LAYER_OPACITY 50, LAYER_BLACK TONE+VIGNETT, LAYER_ZOOM,
  FILM_DAMAGE2, Glitch redblue, the Invert adjustment), `SFX_โหนกระแส 1`, `[BGM]Where Everything Began`,
  `CG_END LOGO`.
- **Not from the template but on every day** (counted on all 13 dailies): **3–4 After Effects Linked
  Comps** (the hook's headline cards), a `Swoosh Transition 03` on each card (3–5), and one
  `Transition Complex 14` (12 of 12 Works days; 0929 has none). The BGM loop gap is 56.48 s on all 13.
  The compilations (EP.162, EP.167) have 1–2 Linked Comps (the show title) and no bed.
- **Never in a daily YT:** the drum set, World Report / The Begin, the lower-third boxes, the
  insert-frame PNGs, the blur covers (the dailies use their own blur adjustment layers). These are
  for other formats (FB, square, compilation) or retired.
- **The compilation shows (EP.162, EP.167)** use the โหนหลังกระแส kit: logo frame over the hook, the AE title
  comp in the 3 s gap after the hook, a `CG BG` card (an opaque 1080x1080 h264; rendered full-frame in
  the agent's import test, so read as a full card, guess) over the case's 2.4–5 s tail after its last line, the long lower
  third restarting at each case (88.62 %, 0.564 / 0.916) with a 2-line case-title Graphic, blur
  boxes over the broadcast logos all through, `CG BG` on the last line, then `CG ENDSCREEN`.
  EP.167 puts the hook stack + FILM_DAMAGE2 on its 2.84 s end tail under `CG BG`; EP.162 uses neither
  (read 2026-11-24). Both mark every edge with a glitch plus a 1–2 frame Invert flash; EP.162's title
  card is the thumbnail PSD animated in After Effects. **16:9 footage is fitted to the width at
  56.25 %, centred, letterboxed** (EP.167 864 of 880 s, EP.162 904 s, read 2026-11-24; an earlier version
  said 100 %, which was wrong). That is why the blur boxes exist: at 56.25 % the broadcast's show bug (top
  left) and its banner logo (bottom left) are in frame, and the template's `Blur LOGO` boxes land on them.

## How it evolved (seen)

2023-08 / 2023-10: only `Plattern_SQUARE SIZE` and `REEL SIZE` (square-first channel). 2024-02: FB
and YT sizes added. 2024-10 → 2026-01: the square pattern is gone; YT and FB are the main outputs.

## What it means (guess)

- Answers the open question on film damage: it belongs to two recipes, **the hook stack and the
  insert frame**: footage the show lifts and frames, not the broadcast's own full-screen pictures.
- The 56.48 s BGM grid is the template's, so it holds on every day; a cut that shortens the bed has
  to keep it (README point 14).
- A draft can be assembled from the kit mechanically: hook stack per headline card, the sting gap,
  the bed on its grid, the end stack and logo. Only the AE headline cards and the text Graphics need
  the user (or an After Effects route).
