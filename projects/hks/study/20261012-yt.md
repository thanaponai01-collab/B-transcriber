## Principles (the why)

1. **Hook = a trial in four lines: accusation, proof, defence, rebuttal** (seen 0–24.7). It sells the
   confrontation, not the premise (guess).
2. **The spine is one provable thread.** Of four victims the cut follows ขวัญ's 36,090 from hook to
   climax, because it has the proof (the nightspot photo/clip), the money trail (Ice's account, 9k
   vegetables) and a live confrontation (M's call) (seen thread; why guess). The largest victim, แม่ฟ้า
   400k+ (src 5380–5993), is dropped whole. Her story is told at an evacuee centre ("เกิดสงคราม…
   ศูนย์พักพิง", src 5921), a different setting, with no confrontation (guess).
3. **A point returns only with new evidence.** "Rushed her, then partied" is told three times:
   ขวัญ's account (253–305), Beer's money trail (629–727), M's call (886–951). Each adds proof, and the
   last gets the admission "แบบเนี้ยพี่ผิด". The pure retellings (T6, T15, T22–T25, break recaps) all go
   (seen). M-4 read as "each *fact* once", not "each *topic* once" (guess).
4. **The accused gets real air** (seen): First's objection runs 153 s (435.6–588.8), his defence
   (790.4–805) and counter-accusation "Beer was drunk" (831.6–854.9) stay whole. Guess: fairness, and
   his own words convict him better than the host does.
5. **Host questions open chapters** (seen): "ยังไงขวัญมันไปโดนยังไงลูก" (184.8), "คุณเปิ้ลไปโดนอะไร" (312.2),
   "คุณคิดว่ามีอะไรที่มันผิดบ้างไหม" (854.9), "ได้ร้อยบาทแล้วคุณกันเอาไว้ไหม" (1110.2). Host *prosecution*
   lines stay ("อย่างเงี้ยไอ้เฟ้อจะพูดยังไง", 889.6); host jokes and summaries go. Agrees with "ask, then
   tell"; it narrows R3 to "cut host *talk*, keep host *questions and charges*" (guess).
6. **Evidence picture whenever words point at an object** (seen timing): photo, poster, sign, post,

7. **Law short and late** (seen 1056.9–1086.4, 30 s), as consequence, not analysis. Same as 20261123.
