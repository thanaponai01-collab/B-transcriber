### The missing principle

**The day's type sets the shape.** A story day (one guest, one conflict) is cut to one proven spine.
An issue day (a disaster, a public problem) is cut as a **briefing**:
- the damage shown first;
- each voice keeps its one best beat, in broadcast order: victim, cause, regulator, each guest's ask;
- the practical takeaways stay as the payoff: insure your real risk, beware places named บาง/คลอง/หนอง,
  prepare even if it turns out for nothing;
- the threat closes.

Why (guess): the viewer of a flood episode wants "what happened, why, what's coming, what do I do";
emotion is spent in the pictures, not the sobs. Check it against 20261001 (the flood explainer) and
20261110 (the foundation-law issue day). If both show the same shape, HKS-3 and HKS-9 become "story
days only", and the briefing rule is the new candidate (HKS-11).
