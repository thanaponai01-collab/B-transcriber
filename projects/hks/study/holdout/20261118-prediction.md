### The missing principle

**Expert talk stays only as the answer to a question about a guest's own case.** Law and police talk
survives only inside a question-and-answer exchange tied to a concrete case on the panel, and the
answer plays right after the question. Expert monologues and general questions go, however relevant.
Evidence in this cut:
- 3770–3824, Tao's case (the questioner is likely the lawyer): the app shows no licence, 1359.go.th
  lists it, "but is the business legal?" → police 3824–3862 "a licence doesn't let you hide package
  fees". The same point, unprompted at 2023–2045 (police) and 3862–3867 / 3932–3949, is cut.
- 4266–4323, Tao: "the police told me I can't report" → police 4323–4375 "police cannot refuse; 1599".
  The same hotline answering a general question ("ถามแทนชาวบ้าน … แจ้งความที่ไหน", 4150–4178, likely
  the lawyer) is cut.
- Every monologue is cut: the lawyer's (3637–3768, 4183–4260) and the police's (1890–1960, 3476–3628,
  4055–4128).

It replaces HKS-9's "law follows the fact": the viewer hears the law when someone like them asks for
it. Two related patterns to check on the other holdouts:
- **Bookend one guest.** The hook is the most surprising guest's mini-arc (Tao), and the ending
  answers that same guest's open question.
- **Don't break a chain of events.** An interruption is moved out of the middle of a cause → effect
  run (the reorder).
