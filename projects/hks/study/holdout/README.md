## What held, what failed

- **Held on all three:** HKS-1 (length), HKS-2 (a hook that the body plays out), HKS-5 (broadcast
  order by default: one move per cut), the host-as-questioner part of HKS-8, and the dropping of
  sponsors and the opening talk.
- **Narrowed:**
  - **HKS-3, one spine:** only story days have a single spine. Issue days (1002) and panels of
    several guests (1118) keep every voice, thinned to its best beat.
  - **HKS-8, the host's verdict:** it was cut in 1109 and kept in 1003, 1012 and 167. An open
    question for the user.
  - **HKS-9, law:** expert talk stays when it answers a question about a guest's own case ("does
    this hit me / her family?", 1109, 1118), even when long. A monologue nobody asked for goes.
  - **HKS-10, the ending:** it closes the lead guest's thread or answers the body's last open
    question (1109, 1118). It did not echo the hook in 2 of 3 days.
- **Predictions that failed on content:** the hook lines picked (1/5 to 2/5 right) and the one
  reorder (0 of 3 right). The hook choice and the single move are where the user's judgment is
  least captured by the list.

## New candidates (from the holdouts, guess)

- **HKS-11, the day's type sets the shape.** A story day is cut to one proven spine. An issue day
  (flood, law) is a briefing: the damage first, each voice's best beat, practical takeaways, the
  threat at the end (1002; fits 1001 and 1110). A panel of several guests keeps every guest, thinned
  (1118).
- **HKS-12, an expert answers a case, never lectures.** Expert or law talk stays only as the answer
  to a question about a guest's own case (1118: the licence check, "police refused my report"; 1109).
- **HKS-13, a party is cleared by evidence and other voices, never by his own pleading** (1109: the
  host is a party; his evidence and others' readings stay, his "I'm not vengeful" and his donation
  go).
