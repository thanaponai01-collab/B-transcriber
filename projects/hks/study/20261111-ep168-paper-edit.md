**A (1.txt) → B (3.txt) → C (2.txt) → D (4.txt). Strongest case last.**

  the episode title's "หมดใจ" echoes D's own title ("รักมิจหมดใจ").
- **The arc escalates the price of love:** money and trust (A: a girlfriend with a husband; B: a
  fiancée and her mother) → a person gone (C) → money, the body and the family threatened (D).
  C's host warning, "สังคมเดี๋ยวนี้มันอันตรายมากกว่าที่เราคิด", hands straight into D.
- **C sits inside, not at an end** (proposal): it has no accused voice, no money, a loose fit to

  strongest-first or strongest-last choice. The case for strongest-last here rests on HKS-15 and

- Alternative: **D first** (hook from D's attack: "บีบคอผู้เสียหาย… กระสุนจะเป็นคำตอบ") and end
