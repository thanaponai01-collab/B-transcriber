Card boundaries are the agent's reading of the line order (2 lines a card, as rendered on 1123).

## How the user writes them (seen across 13 days)

1. **Two lines a card: a punch, then the fact.** Line 1 is short and loaded, often ending in "!" or
   opening with a verb of exposure (แฉ, เปิดโปง, ชำแหละ, งัด, โป๊ะแตก); line 2 carries who / what / how
   much.
2. **The three or four cards are the whole arc**: setup → escalation → the worst detail or the turn → the
   climax, the twist or the open question. Story days end on the most visceral fact (1003 the cage,
   1123 the stabbing) or on the turn (1120 "the case may flip", 1109 the host's proof, 1012 the
   denial). Issue days end on the authority's promise or warning (1110, 1002).
3. **A number on almost every day** (5 แสน, 2 ปีครึ่ง, 100 บาท, 3 ล้าน, 1,600 → 2,800, 700 สาย, 14,000
   แห่ง, 300 ล้าน vs 5 ล้าน). Contrasts are set side by side (1,600 borrowed / 2,800 repaid; 300 M lost /
   5 M insured).
4. **The other side's words go in quotes** ("ผมไม่เคยกันเงินทำบุญ", "เราจะไม่ทิ้งใครไว้ข้างหลัง",
   "ช่วยผมด้วยครับ"), and names in quotes too ("น้ำ รพีภัทร", "บุ๋ม ปนัดดา").
5. **The cards tell what the voice does not** (1123 study point 1): the voice under them plays one or
   two emotional lines; the cards carry the plot.

## Why it matters for drafts (guess)

A draft can propose the four cards from the paper edit: one card per act, a number where the story
has one, the accused's line in quotes, the last card the turn or the worst fact. The text then goes
to the user for the After Effects comp. The After Effects MCP server cannot find `AfterFX.exe`
(`AE_MCP_EXE` unset), but `AfterFX.exe -r <script.jsx>` plus `aerender` work (2026-11-24: the EP.168
compilation title card was built that way, [20260928-ep162-compilation.md](20260928-ep162-compilation.md)).
The daily cards are live AE text layers, so the same route could set their text; not tried yet.
