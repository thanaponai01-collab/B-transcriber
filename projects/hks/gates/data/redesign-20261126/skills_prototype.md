source. Three designs:

## Verdict

One per stage (b), for the build. Each stage loads 9 to 44% of (a), and the stage's rules and
questions sit next to the work. Over a full day it loads about as much as (a) or (c). On 1125
compaction decided nothing either way, so the measured case for (b) is distance alone: the build
rules sat 158k or more tokens back under (c), against at most 71k under (b).

The handover is where (b) is weakest. Its own 25 questions are close, but the other 129 are 78k to
277k back, and the day-setup questions are as far as under (c). If the written answers matter
most, `hks-handover` should also carry all 154 questions, about 5.6k more Claude tokens (inferred),
or `premiere_hks_handover` should return the missing ids with their questions.
