# Club Friday (CFD) principles (the why)

Read [the main principles](../PRINCIPLES.md) first; where this file departs from them, this file wins.

**IDs:** `CFD-<n>`. Rules and code cite these IDs.

## Principles

**CFD-1. Keep the full story.** Each clip is already a finished video; we only trim the show's opening
and ending, never the caller's story or the hosts' answer inside it. *(confirmed, 2026-11-26: "I would
cut the first and the end part out, which like the opening and the ending, so we keep the full story")*

**CFD-2. The advice is the takeaway, so it goes on screen in the host's own words.** Quote the host's
exact advice, not a paraphrase. *(confirmed, 2026-11-26: "put the text on top of that video, quote
their exact advice")*

**CFD-3. Quote what the hosts really give, at most one per host.** Normally one advice per host (two
hosts → two quotes), but don't invent a second: sometimes only one host gives advice, and sometimes the
hosts only encourage the caller or give information, with no advice at all. Then the clip gets **no
text** (confirmed 2026-11-26: "No text then"). *(confirmed, 2026-11-26: "normally I would do one advice per host";
"sometimes there is only one advice that is fine as well … sometimes just like encourage … maybe
sometimes no advice at all, just for information")* Why one each: *(guess: every host gets a voice,
and one line stays readable; ask)*

**CFD-4. Every clip with advice gets its advice text.** No advice in a clip is left without its quote;
a clip with no advice gets none. *(confirmed, 2026-11-26: "if there's a 10 video, it needs to have
text every video"; narrowed the same day: no advice → "No text then")*

**CFD-5. A Short is sold by its story; the advice is the payoff.** Pick the clips whose story stands
out (most dramatic, or unlike the others) and whose caller says something that makes the viewer ask a
question, so they keep watching. The one advice comes around the end, where people would otherwise
leave. *(confirmed, 2026-11-26: "not strongest advice, but strongest story, like really drama or
really unique"; "some words that the case speaks that quite catch … gain retention"; "advice comes
around the end … just keep the shorts interesting")* Related: the watch-time candidate in
[PRINCIPLES.md](../PRINCIPLES.md).
