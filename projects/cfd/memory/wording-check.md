- **Start from the clip's word timings**, then compare the span with 2–3 other Thai models (CFD 99 used
  pathumma-large-v3, typhoon-large-v3, whisper-th-medium). Where they agree against the engine, take theirs.
  CFD 99 examples: ปฏิเหตุ→อุบัติเหตุ, กว่าเขา→ของเขา, เห็นคุย→แอบคุย, เจ๊ข้อลูก→เจคลอดลูก (her name is เจ). *(seen)*
- **Drop fillers** (นะคะ, อ่ะเนาะ, ปุ๊บ, อะค่ะ) but keep every content word. Subtitles are full and exact, never
  shortened (CFD-R8). *(confirmed for subtitles; fillers seen, the user didn't object)*
- **Where models disagree or a stretch is unclear,** pick the likeliest and **name it to the user** with its time,
  or leave it out and say so. Never present guessed words as heard. CFD 99 doubts: Short 3 at 230.5 "16 ปี",
  the name "คิม", "พี่อ้อย พี่ชอบ" left out.
- Nothing has been checked by ear by the agent; the user hears it in Premiere and re-times or fixes it.

**Why it matters:** wrong words on screen are the worst kind of mistake for a talk show.
**How to apply:** every titles or captions handover lists the doubtful words. Related: [[shorts-captions]], [[cold-open-titles]]
