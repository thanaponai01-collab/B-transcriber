Every 1RaceClub project has: V1 = YT stream layout (4 audio streams: 1 voice, 2 duplicate, 3–4 silent),
V2 = only me (phone cam recorded sideways, rotated in the project), V3 = me with data (never used).
**How to apply:** building a sequence, keep all three layers, disable V3 and A2–A6, zero gaps. Full
Shorts layer map: [shorts-rules.md](../shorts-rules.md) section 9.
