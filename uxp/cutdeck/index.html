ADJ & FX Tools & Shortcuts Guide
Adjustment Layer:
Click Span 1 continuous AL over full selection
Ctrl+Click Place 1 separate AL per selected clip (e.g. 3 clips = 3 ALs)
⇧ Shift+Click 50/50 cut transition on every cut between selected clips (or at playhead)
Quick-effect buttons (below Adjustment Layer):
Click Span 1 AL over the selection and apply that preset's effect to it
Ctrl+Click 1 AL per selected clip, each with that preset's effect applied
⇧ Shift+Click 50/50 cut transition AL(s) with that preset's effect applied
Capture (Settings) Name it, then save the selected clip/AL's real applied effects as a new quick-effect button
Edit (Settings, below Capture) Rename a preset in its text field, or remove it with ×
Settings Reference:
• Transition (50/50): Frame duration split evenly across the cut point (e.g. 16f = 8f left + 8f right).
• Clamp: Shrinks transition on short clips to prevent bleeding into adjacent cuts.
• Bin & Color: Target bin folder & timeline label color for created ALs.
